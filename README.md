## Hand It Off

![Hand It Off cover](books/hand-it-off/cover.jpg)

**Hand It Off**  
A Practical Guide to Grok Bot, from Your First Task to a Team That Works While You Sleep

By Michael Gannotti  
Published by SMF Works  
Final edition, 11 November 2026

Hand the work off; keep the yes.

A warm, practical guide to Grok Bot, written as a free gift: how to hand off real work to a teammate that has a computer, from your first task to a small team that keeps going after you close the laptop. Michael Gannotti walks through getting in, connecting apps and the Vault, memory, skills, routines, a digital chief of staff, growth and shop-floor work, a publishing-house fleet, household admin and errands, Team Bots and group chats, and the safety, privacy, and money rules that have to stay yours. Every how-to says what “done” looks like and what still needs your yes. You are still the one who says yes to send, post, pay, delete, publish, or merge. The book is independent and unofficial.

~6.5 hours · 263 pages · 94,455 words
